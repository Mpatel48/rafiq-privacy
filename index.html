Rafiq privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Who we are

Rafiq is made and published by ATMZ FOREVER LLC ("we", "us"). Rafiq helps Muslim men leave pornography behind, with daily lessons, an urge helper, check-ins and an optional website filter. You don't need an account. We don't run servers for Rafiq, and the app has no analytics, tracking or ads.

What is stored on your phone

Rafiq keeps everything you enter on your phone, in the app's private storage: your answers from the first chat (your reasons and hard times), your nightly check-ins and the "first step in the chain" notes after a slip, the urges you log and what triggered them, the thoughts you untangle, the journal pages you write (and the mood you pick), the lessons you finish, the prayers you mark, and your settings. Rafiq's answers to what you write are worked out on the phone, without internet, unless you use the optional AI helper described below. Backups are turned off for Rafiq, so this data is left out of Android's cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfers. We never receive any of it.

If you add an AI key, it is encrypted with a key held in Android's secure Keystore and stays on the phone.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, Android checks your fingerprint, face or phone PIN. Rafiq only learns whether the check passed; it never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN. Rafiq also asks Android to hide its screens from screenshots and the recent-apps view.

The Shield (website filter, uses Android's VPN)

The Shield is off until you turn it on. It uses Android's VpnService to set up a VPN that stays on your phone and carries only website-name lookups (DNS). All other traffic goes the normal way and is never seen by Rafiq.

Each website name your phone looks up (for example "example.com") is checked against a small list built into the app, and is then sent, encrypted (DNS over HTTPS), to CleanBrowsing's family filter, with Cloudflare's family filter as the backup, which reply whether the site is safe. If neither can be reached, the lookup is sent to the same two filters without encryption. Adult sites get "no such site". Only the name is sent: never the pages you visit, what you read or what you type. CleanBrowsing and Cloudflare handle these lookups under their own privacy policies (cleanbrowsing.org/privacy, cloudflare.com/privacypolicy). Rafiq doesn't save the names.

Optional AI helper (Google Gemini)

The AI helper is off until you add your own Gemini API key. While a key is added, only in "Untangle a thought", the words you type in that chat (the situation, the thought and the kinder thought) are sent directly from your phone to Google's Gemini API, with your key, so Rafiq can answer them. Nothing else is sent: not your check-ins, urges, prayers, reasons, journal or anything from other screens. The AI's replies are not saved.

This request goes from your phone to Google; we don't see it. Google handles it under the Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy. On Google's free tier, Google may use what is sent to improve its products, and people at Google may review it, so leave out names and anything that could identify you. Tap Remove key on the You screen to stop at any time.

Watch app

If you install Rafiq on a Wear OS watch, the phone sends it a small summary through Google Play services' Wear Data Layer, straight between your own phone and watch: this month's clean nights, today's check-in and prayers, your reasons and a few ayat for the urge helper. The watch sends back the check-ins, prayers and urges you mark on it. No Rafiq server is involved.

Sharing your week

If you tap Share on the weekly card, Rafiq makes a picture of your week (nights of light, waves ridden, lanterns lit; never slips) and hands it to the app you choose in Android's share sheet. Rafiq sends nothing by itself.

Permissions

VPN (asked by Android when you turn on the Shield): the website filter described above.

Notifications (optional, asked when you turn on a reminder): the night reminder and the medication reminder.

Internet: the Shield's lookups and, only with your key, the AI helper.

Run at startup: turns the Shield and your reminders back on after a restart or an update, if you had them on.

What we don't do

We don't collect, sell or share your information. We don't receive your check-ins, urges, thoughts, journal, prayers, reasons, your AI key or the websites you look up.

Children

Rafiq is meant for adults (18 and older) and is not directed at children.

Deleting your information

Remove your AI key on the You screen. Delete a journal page by opening it and tapping Delete. Clearing Rafiq's storage in Android's settings (Settings > Apps > Rafiq > Storage > Clear storage), or uninstalling the app, deletes everything it stored on your phone. Uninstalling Rafiq from the watch deletes the summary kept there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version comes with an app update and is posted online with a new effective date.
